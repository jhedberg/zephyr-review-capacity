How this is counted

  • An area is a section of MAINTAINERS.yml; its pull requests are those carrying the area's own label. Areas that share a label share their pull requests.
  • A request counts once per person and pull request, on the day of the first request. It counts as answered when the person reviews at or after the request and before the end of the period, or comments if that option is on.
  • Not counted for or against anyone: the person's own pull requests, requests withdrawn without an answer, pull requests closed with less than three ready days after the request, and open ones with less than seven ready days since the request. Draft time is not ready time.
  • With the excuse option on, a miss is also not counted when the pull request closed within seven days of the request or two other people had approved it within seven days.
  • People with fewer than five countable requests are not judged. The line never takes a Maintainer off.
  • Approved counts the pull requests the person first approved in the period, asked or not. Bot accounts are left out everywhere.
  • A regular reviewer is a listed person who answers at least half of five or more requests in the period. An occasional reviewer took part but is not regular; a silent one did not take part; with fewer than five requests there is too little to tell. A pull request is not covered when fewer than two regular reviewers are left besides its author. An area whose label is named after another area (a West project using "area: Tests", say) sees that area's pull requests, so its figures are not its own; such areas are hidden there unless asked for.
  • A period that includes a holiday season lowers everyone's rate. Compare like with like before drawing a conclusion about a person.
  • The numbers come from public GitHub data and are refreshed by a script; see the source for the exact rules.
